RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT
This Residential Tenancy Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of January 2026 at Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

BETWEEN:
Mr. K. Ramachandra Murthy, residing at Flat 402, Green Meadows, Madhapur, Hyderabad - 500081 (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR / LANDLORD", which expression shall include his heirs, executors, and assigns);

AND:
Ms. Ananya Sharma, working at Cyber Gateway, Madhapur, Hyderabad - 500081 (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE / TENANT", which expression shall include her legal representatives).

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 301, Sri Sai Residency, Kondapur, Hyderabad, comprising 2 Bedrooms, Hall, Kitchen (approx. 1250 sq. ft.) ("Scheduled Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TENURE AND DURATION
The tenancy shall be for an initial period of eleven (11) months commencing from January 1, 2026, expiring on November 30, 2026. The Agreement may be renewed upon mutual consent with a 7% escalation in monthly rent.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND MAINTENANCE CHARGES
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of INR 32,000/- (Rupees Thirty Two Thousand only) payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every calendar month via direct NEFT/UPI bank transfer. In case of delay beyond the 10th of the month, a late fee of INR 500/- per week shall apply. Society maintenance charges of INR 3,500/- per month shall be paid directly by the Tenant to the Resident Welfare Association.

3. INTEREST-FREE REFUNDABLE SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has deposited an Interest-Free Refundable Security Deposit of INR 1,60,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Sixty Thousand only, equivalent to 5 months' rent) via bank transfer. The Landlord agrees to refund the said deposit in full within seven (7) banking days after the Tenant vacates the Scheduled Premises, subject to inspection and deduction for unpaid utility bills or structural damage beyond normal wear and tear.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Tenant shall maintain the interior fixtures, electrical switches, and plumbing faucets in good tenantable order. Minor routine repairs costing up to INR 1,500/- shall be borne by the Tenant. Major structural repairs, seepage, or electrical main line faults shall be rectified by the Landlord within 14 days of written notice.

5. INSPECTION AND QUIET ENJOYMENT
The Landlord or his authorized representative shall have the right to inspect the premises during reasonable daylight hours, subject to providing at least twenty-four (24) hours prior written notice to the Tenant. The Landlord covenants that the Tenant paying the rent shall enjoy peaceful possession without unlawful disturbance.

6. RESTRICTIONS ON USE AND SUB-LETTING
The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and her immediate family. The Tenant shall not sub-let, assign, or part with possession of any portion of the premises to any third party. Keeping pets or running commercial consultancy from the flat is strictly prohibited without prior written consent.

7. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this Agreement prior to expiry of the 11-month term by giving two (2) months' advance written notice or two months' rent in lieu of notice. However, neither party shall terminate the agreement during the initial lock-in period of six (6) months.

8. JURISDICTION
In the event of any disputes regarding deposit refund, eviction, or damages, the parties agree to submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts and Rent Authority at Hyderabad, Telangana.
